Going live: what it takes, and what you can launch for free

KenganSocial · MiraiStack · 25 October 2026 · Prepared from the code as it stands today and current provider terms. Rand figures use R17.50 per US$ and include VAT where it applies; check live prices before paying anything.

Short answer: yes, you can launch a free first version without spending money. A "KenganSocial Beta" can run on a free cloud server with free web hosting and free AI tiers. Creators would get the full video editor, captions, clips from their own uploads, AI Edit, green screen and AI background removal, the music library and the content calendar.

What the free version can't do yet: take payments (not needed for a free beta), publish to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok for the public (those need platform approval, which costs nothing but takes weeks), or use the paid extras: voiceovers, AI music, AI images and fast cloud transcription for long videos.

The trade-off is capacity. A free server and free AI are slow and rate-limited, so plan for a closed beta of about 20 to 50 invited creators, not an open launch.

Path 1 · Free beta

R0 a month

  • About 20–50 invited testers, one video job at a time
  • Editor, captions, clips, AI Edit, green screen, background removal, music, calendar
  • Publishing: YouTube (up to 100 listed users) and LinkedIn
  • No payments; everyone gets a free beta plan
  • Ready in about 1–2 weeks of setup

Path 2 · Full paid launch

≈ R3,050–R5,060 a month fixed

  • Open sign-up, paid plans through Payfast
  • Every feature, including voiceovers, AI music, AI images, launch videos and video ads
  • Publishing to all platforms once approved (4–12 weeks)
  • Per-use AI costs are covered by plan prices (about 65–68% margin)
  • Fixed costs are covered by roughly 17–29 paying Creator Starter subscribers

Checklist: the free launch

Free = costs nothing. Me = I can do this part for you; you only need to approve or log in.

  1. Create an Oracle Cloud account and a free server FreeOracle's "Always Free" tier gives an ARM server; it was reportedly cut in June 2026 from 4 cores / 24 GB to 2 cores / 12 GB, plus free disk. It needs a bank card for identity checks but doesn't charge it. Choose the Johannesburg region (keeps data in South Africa for POPIA); if Johannesburg says "out of capacity", retry later or pick another region and note it in the privacy policy.
  2. Make the app run on that server MeOracle's free server uses ARM processors. I'll check every part (API, video service, Chrome rendering, the background-removal model) builds for ARM, then start the stack with Docker and HTTPS. One render at a time on 2 cores.
  3. Host the website for free Free MeUse Firebase Hosting (already configured in the project) or Cloudflare Pages. Not Vercel's free plan: it doesn't allow commercial use.
  4. Point your domain at it FreeIn GoDaddy DNS: kengansocial.co.za and www to the website, api to the Oracle server. You already own the domain; just keep its renewal paid.
  5. Get a free Gemini API key FreeFrom Google AI Studio. It's faster than the built-in free fallback. On the free tier Google may use prompts to improve its models, so don't send confidential client material, and say so in the privacy policy (next items).
  6. Get a free Pexels key FreeFor stock videos in the editor and lyric video backgrounds.
  7. Set the server to free mode MeNo paid keys; local transcription only (CLIP_TRANSCRIBE=local); free AI fallback on; YouTube link import off; paid-only buttons (AI lyrics, voiceovers, AI images, AI music) hidden or marked "coming soon".
  8. Give every tester a "Beta" plan MeToday, new sign-ups land on the Free plan, which allows no clips or AI. I'll add a Beta plan given at sign-up, with limits sized to the free server (for example: videos up to 15 minutes, 3 clip projects and 10 exports a month).
  9. Set up YouTube and LinkedIn developer apps FreeYouTube: OAuth consent screen in "Testing" with your testers' emails listed (up to 100). LinkedIn: "Share on LinkedIn" product. Rotate the YouTube client secret first: part of it appeared in a log earlier.
  10. Replace the exposed keys FreeDelete the Firebase service-account key starting ac504d10 and make a new one, set a fresh TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY and FREE_CLIP_SECRET, and check the TikTok key (it's currently the same as the secret, which looks like a paste mistake).
  11. Publish the Firebase rules and indexes FreeNeeds your owner login: firebase deploy --only firestore:rules,firestore:indexes. The service account I have can't do it.
  12. Update the privacy policy for the beta MeSay which providers process data abroad (Google Gemini free tier, the Pollinations fallback, Openverse) and that it's a beta.
  13. Register an Information Officer (POPIA) FreeOnline with the Information Regulator. Required before you process people's personal information, even for free.
  14. Make the support addresses work Freesupport@ and privacy@kengansocial.co.za: forward them to your Gmail with Cloudflare Email Routing or Zoho Mail's free plan.
  15. Backups FreeOracle's free tier includes disk backups; schedule a weekly one. Firestore's automatic exports need a paid Google plan, so take a manual export before big changes.
  16. Test on the live server, then invite testers MeRun the deployment check and the end-to-end tests against the real domain, then invite your first 10 creators, collect feedback in one form, and grow to 20–50.
Know the limits of free. Free tiers can change or run out without notice (Oracle has already cut its allowance once this year). A 2-core server renders slowly: a 10-minute video may take 15–30 minutes to turn into clips, and testers queue behind each other. Treat the beta as a test, and move to Part B once people would pay.

Checklist: before charging money

  1. Register the business, if MiraiStack isn't registered yet Small feeA (Pty) Ltd at CIPC. Meta's business verification, Payfast and a business bank account all ask for it.
  2. Move to the paid server ≈ R2,450/month MeRe-open Google Cloud billing (both accounts are closed), create the server, move the data across from the beta server.
  3. Open Payfast and test a full subscription No upfront cost MeSandbox first: subscribe, renew, cancel, failed payment. Then one real low-value payment and refund.
  4. Add the paid AI and media keys Pay per useOpenRouter first (biggest quality jump), then ElevenLabs, Treblo, Perplexity as you switch those features on.
  5. Submit the platform reviews early FreeMeta, TikTok and Google take 4–12 weeks, so start during the beta. The screencast scripts are in docs/SOCIAL_SETUP.md and docs/API_APPLICATIONS.md.
  6. Legal before the first payment Advice feePAIA manual, ECTA disclosures on the website, Terms and Privacy Policy reviewed by someone qualified, POPIA agreements with each provider that handles user data.
  7. Turn on automatic backups and monitoring Included aboveScheduled Firestore exports, daily disk snapshots, an uptime check on /api/health/ready; test a restore once.
  8. Store media in the cloud when you grow MeFor the first paying users, files on the server's disk are fine. Past a few hundred users, move them to cloud storage so the server can be replaced or scaled.
