1,000 users at launch with fewer AI features

KenganSocial · 26 October 2026 · from scripts/capacity-model.mjs (prices and limits from scripts/pricing-model.mjs). Re-run node scripts/build-launch-ai-doc.mjs after changes.

Recommendation: launch with the core AI plus cloud transcription (LAUNCH_AI_FEATURES=cloudTranscription, also the default when the setting is left out). For 1,000 users at typical use this cuts AI API spend from R21 004 to R14 141 a month (33% less), keeps clips arriving in about 6 min, and needs no extra servers. Switching cloud transcription off too saves a further R6 732 but makes long-video clips take ~37 min at the busiest hour and needs 10 clip servers instead of 3.

  • AI credits cost less per credit in launch mode because credits can only buy text (~$0.006 a credit) instead of images, lyrics and website analysis (~$0.009 a credit on average).
